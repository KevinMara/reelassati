Open-source notices

The optional video export engine runs FFmpeg as a separate WebAssembly worker. It includes unmodified @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10 (GPL-2.0-or-later), the MIT-licensed @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15 wrapper, and DejaVu Sans.

The FFmpeg component is distributed under GNU GPL version 3, as permitted by its “or later” license. It is provided without warranty. The original GPL version 2 text is also available.

Corresponding source and build instructions

Complete upstream sources, modifications, bindings, and build scripts are available without charge from the links below. The upstream Dockerfile identifies the toolchain and linked library versions. REELassati changes no FFmpeg source; it compresses the distributed binary for delivery and bundles the MIT worker wrapper. The binary is delivered at core.bin (gzip), with its loader at core.js.

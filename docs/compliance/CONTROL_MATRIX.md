# EU AI Act control matrix

**Matrix version:** `eu-ai-act-2026-08-04.v1`

**Verified against official sources through:** 4 August 2026

**Stable-ID rule:** IDs are never reused. Retired controls remain in this file
with a replacement reference and decision record.

This matrix is an engineering and governance control map. It does not certify
the product, decide disputed legal questions, or replace advice from qualified
counsel. “Implemented” means a source-level mechanism exists; it does not mean
that the deployed configuration, upstream evidence, operations, or every use
case has been verified.

The code-facing evidence anchors below describe intended control locations.
They do not close a row merely by existing. As of 4 August 2026, operator/date
facts, upstream-provider evidence, marking/detection interoperability and
lifecycle evidence, named incident/monitoring evidence, AI-literacy records,
and related-law review remain open public-launch blockers. See the dated status
table in `README.md`.

## Status vocabulary

- **Active:** required for the current intended product or route.
- **Conditional:** required only when the recorded facts trigger it.
- **Open blocker:** a required fact, implementation, or evidence item is absent.
- **Monitor:** presently outside scope or dependent on pending official work,
  but must be reassessed on a trigger.
- **Not currently applicable:** supported by documented current facts, not a
  permanent exemption.

## Control dependencies

- `TRN-002` and `TRN-003` are a pair. Neither may be marked complete alone.
- `PRV-001` is the source evidence for `TRN-002`, `TRN-003`, `PUB-001`, and
  `PUB-002`; a client-side field alone is insufficient.
- `PUB-001` must complete before `PUB-002` creates an immutable outgoing intent.
- `PUB-004` verifies the real publication surface; a correct internal review
  does not guarantee that a platform preserved the disclosure.
- `SUP-001` supports, but never replaces, REELassati's system-provider controls.
- `UX-001` constrains presentation, not legal scope: compact disclosures still
  must satisfy `TRN-004`, `TRN-006`, and `TRN-008` where triggered.
- `GOV-003`, `TRN-002`, `TRN-003`, `PUB-001`, `PUB-002`, `LIT-001`, and
  `EVD-001` are public-release blockers unless a written, legally reviewed
  non-applicability decision exists.

## Minimum release evidence bundle

For each release affecting any mapped control, preserve:

1. release/version identifier and policy version;
2. completed `CHANGE_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md` with affected control IDs;
3. actual environment route/model names without secret values;
4. provider-evidence revision and unresolved provider gaps;
5. provenance/marking/detection test results for every affected output type;
6. first-interaction and disclosure screenshots for both themes/locales where
   applicable;
7. publication review, invalidation, outbox, and ambiguous-delivery test results;
8. accessibility and reduced-motion results for changed surfaces;
9. named reviewers, date, open limitations, remediation owner and due date;
10. legal approval where role, intended use, rights, privacy, or public claims
    changed.

Do not choose an arbitrary evidence-retention period. Set and document one only
after legal, privacy, contractual, security, and operational requirements have
been reconciled.
